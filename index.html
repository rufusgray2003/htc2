HIT THE CYCLIST 2

Whack the cyclist. Then become the cyclist.

Tap to whack the cyclist off the cliff: dead centre between the grass patches goes furthest.
Then hold the left or right side of the screen to steer onto molehills. Don't land too steep!
Tap your powerup slots (bottom right) to use them.

1 · Hitter

You're at the top of a cliff. A cyclist rides toward the edge, through two grass patches.
Swing as they ride through: early sends them higher (risky), late sends them flatter and faster.
Dead centre, between the patches, goes furthest and earns a free 🚀.
Miss, and they ride off the edge anyway, slow and steep.

2 · Cyclist

You're knocked off the bike and bounce along the dirt on your belly.
Steer with A / D to land on molehills. Hit them in a row for a bigger launch each time.
Come down from too high, or too steeply, or hit a rock, and you'll splut head-first. That ends the run!
Mud slows you, arrows speed you up.
Grab ? boxes on the ground for powerups. Fly through low clouds: some hide a 🪂 wingsuit.
Stop near a molehill and its owner will let you know how they feel.
10 easter eggs are hidden out there. Crash into them for a surprise…
Click the follow cam (bottom left) to enlarge it.

Powerups

Rare! Only from ? boxes lying on the ground, a perfect hit, or a 5-molehill chain.
Use them with 1 2 3 whenever you like.
